# Payment Observability

Payment observability emits safe operational signals only.

## Metrics

- Payment intent count.
- Payment success count.
- Payment failure count.
- Refund count.
- Webhook failure count.
- Webhook duplicate count.
- Payment latency.
- Provider latency.
- Revenue metrics.

## Dashboards

Dashboards should show success rate, refund rate, failed payment rate, gross revenue, net revenue, provider latency, and webhook health.
