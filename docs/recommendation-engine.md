# Recommendation Engine

The Recommendation Engine turns SALORA business data into personalized product, pairing, upsell, and loyalty suggestions.

## Inputs

- Product catalog
- Customer preferences
- Order history
- Loyalty tier and points
- Inventory availability
- Time of day

## Outputs

- Ranked product recommendations
- Pairing recommendations
- Dessert and add-on upsells
- Loyalty reward suggestions

## Production Notes

The engine works with the mock provider and does not require external AI credentials. Provider-backed language can explain recommendations, but ranking remains deterministic and testable.
