# Operations Intelligence Analysis

SALORA now has orders, payments, customers, loyalty, AI runtime, recommendations, WhatsApp, notifications, inventory, and revenue infrastructure. The remaining gap is executive visibility: operators need aggregated intelligence instead of inspecting raw APIs or logs.

## Operational Blind Spots

- Orders and payments exist, but no single operational status summary exists.
- Inventory movements can be recorded, but risk scoring is not centralized.
- AI evaluation and payment metrics exist, but there is no executive health score.
- WhatsApp and notifications have channel metrics, but no dashboard contract.

## Reporting Gaps

- No daily, weekly, or monthly KPI package.
- No customer health scoring.
- No loyalty engagement summary.
- No alerting engine for operations, revenue, inventory, AI, and WhatsApp degradation.

## Intelligence Gaps

- Revenue analytics are foundational but need health scores.
- Customer analytics need repeat purchase and churn-risk readiness.
- AI analytics need quality, safety, and cost-efficiency scoring.
- Forecasting requires clean aggregate snapshots before ML models are useful.

## Growth Opportunities

- Improve retention through loyalty engagement tracking.
- Improve conversion by linking paid orders to recommendations.
- Improve operational response with alerting before incidents become customer-facing.
- Build executive dashboards from stable backend contracts.

## Executive KPI Requirements

- Gross revenue, net revenue, AOV, refund rate, success rate.
- Customer count, repeat purchase readiness, churn risk, LTV readiness.
- Loyalty points activity and reward readiness.
- AI usage quality, cost efficiency, safety score, recommendation effectiveness.
- Orders, payment failures, inventory risks, WhatsApp failures, notification queue state.

## Recommended Strategy

Create analytics subdomains, intelligence scoring services, KPI engine, alerting foundation, forecasting-ready snapshots, and secured read-only APIs. Avoid full UI work in this phase.
